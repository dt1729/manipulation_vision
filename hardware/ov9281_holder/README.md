# OV9281 camera holder (v2)

Closed, two-part holder for a 40 × 40 mm OV9281 MIPI board (UC-788 Rev.B).

**Status: not printed or test-fitted yet.** Board size and hole spacing come from the
datasheet. The 1.6 mm board thickness, 5 mm of room for back-side parts, and the lens
pointing towards the foot side are assumptions, so check them against your board.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | 3D viewer: open in a browser, works offline. The green board is a plain placeholder |
| `ov9281_holder_v2_base.stl` | Base with foot and hex nut pockets |
| `ov9281_holder_v2_cover.stl` | Back cover with a cable slot |

## Assembly

1. Press 4 M2 nuts into the pockets on the base's inner face. They are 4.3 mm across flats.
2. Lay the board on the base with the lens through the opening and the ribbon connector at the top.
3. Fit the cover and tighten 4 M2 × 10 mm bolts from the back until just snug.

The foot has 2 Ø3.4 mm holes, 17 mm apart, for M3. Print the base inner face down and the
cover open side up.
